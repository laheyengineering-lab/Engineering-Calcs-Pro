Engineering References

Sources cited on the individual calculator pages, grouped by type. Calculator-specific citations remain on each calculator page; this page gives broader context.

How to use this page

Entries are reproduced from the references section of the calculator pages listed beside them. Citations are given as they appear there; where no edition or year is listed, use the edition appropriate to your project. Being listed here does not mean a source supports every formula in a calculator, and it does not mean the calculator implements the complete standard. Check the calculator’s own page for how each source is used.

Standards

Standards are cited by designation. Obtain the current edition from the publisher; the links go to publisher home pages.

  • ISO 281, Rolling bearings — Dynamic load ratings and rating life. Used by: Bearing Life.
  • ISO 262, ISO general-purpose metric screw threads — Selected sizes for screws, bolts and nuts. Used by: Thread Strength.
  • ISO 898-1, Mechanical properties of fasteners made of carbon steel and alloy steel — Bolts, screws and studs. Used by: Thread Strength.
  • AISC: ANSI/AISC 360, Specification for Structural Steel Buildings, Chapter J2, Welds and Welded Joints. Used by: Fillet Weld (directional-strength factor). Also cited for context in Thread Strength (Chapter J; not implemented there).
  • AWS D1.1/D1.1M, Structural Welding Code – Steel. Cited by Fillet Weld for fabrication and procedure requirements, which are separate from that calculation.
  • DIN 7190-1, Interference fits – Calculation and design rules for cylindrical self-holding interference fits. Cited by Press Fit.

Machine design and fasteners

  • Budynas, R. G., & Nisbett, J. K. Shigley’s Mechanical Engineering Design. Cited by: Fillet Weld (effective throat), Press Fit (press and shrink fits; stresses in cylinders), Springs (helical springs and spring stress).
  • Shigley, J. E., Mischke, C. R., & Budynas, R. G. Mechanical Engineering Design. Cited by: Bolt Torque (fastener preload concepts), Shaft Torsion (shaft design and torsion), Factor of Safety.
  • Wahl, A. M. Mechanical Springs, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill, 1963. Cited by: Springs.
  • SAE International, SAE HS-795, Spring Design Manual. Cited by: Springs.
  • NASA Reference Publication 1228, Fastener Design Manual. Cited by: Thread Strength (fastener design background and thread failure considerations).
  • SKF Group, Rolling Bearings Catalogue. Cited by: Bearing Life (rating life methodology and application factors).
  • Harris, T. A., & Kotzalas, M. N. Rolling Bearing Analysis. Cited by: Bearing Life.

Mechanics of materials, structures, and statics

  • Hibbeler, R. C. Mechanics of Materials. Cited by: Beam Bending Stress, Stress & Strain, Thermal Expansion (thermal strain and constrained stress).
  • Beer, F. P., Johnston, E. R., DeWolf, J. T., & Mazurek, D. F. Mechanics of Materials. Cited by: Beam Bending Stress, Stress & Strain.
  • Roark, R. J., Young, W. C., & Budynas, R. G. Roark’s Formulas for Stress and Strain. Cited by: Beam Bending Stress, Beam Deflection.
  • Timoshenko, S. P., & Gere, J. M. Theory of Elastic Stability. Cited by: Column Buckling.
  • Hibbeler, R. C. Engineering Mechanics: Statics; Beer, F. P., Johnston, E. R., Mazurek, D. F., et al. Vector Mechanics for Engineers: Statics; Meriam, J. L., & Kraige, L. G. Engineering Mechanics: Statics. Cited by: Moment.

Materials and thermal background

  • Callister, W. D., & Rethwisch, D. G. Materials Science and Engineering. Cited by: Stress & Strain (elastic behavior context).
  • Incropera, F. P., DeWitt, D. P., et al. Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer. Cited by: Thermal Expansion.
  • ASME B31 piping design references. Cited by: Thermal Expansion as context for thermal expansion in piping systems. The specific B31 part and edition are not identified on that page, and this calculator does not implement B31.
