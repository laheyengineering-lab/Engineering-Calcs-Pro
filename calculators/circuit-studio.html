      Worked example

      Given

      Connect \(R_1=100\ \Omega\) and \(R_2=300\ \Omega\) to a 12 V ideal source. Evaluate both Series and Parallel modes.

      Find

      Find equivalent resistance, source and individual currents, resistor voltage drops and power. Check conservation in both layouts.

      Solution

      Series. Current is common to the two resistors.

      \[ R_s=R_1+R_2=100+300=400\ \Omega \]
      \[ I_1=I_2=I_s=\frac{12}{400}=0.030\ \mathrm{A} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}V_1&=I_sR_1=(0.030)(100)=3\ \mathrm{V}\\ V_2&=I_sR_2=(0.030)(300)=9\ \mathrm{V}\\ V_1+V_2&=3+9=12\ \mathrm{V}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}P_1&=V_1 I_s=(3)(0.030)=0.09\ \mathrm{W}\\ P_2&=V_2 I_s=(9)(0.030)=0.27\ \mathrm{W}\\ P_s&=0.09+0.27=0.36\ \mathrm{W}\end{aligned} \]

      The drops sum to the source voltage, and \(P_s=12(0.030)=0.36\ \mathrm{W}\) matches the source power delivered.

      Parallel. Each branch has \(V_1=V_2=12\ \mathrm{V}\).

      \[ \begin{aligned}R_p&=\left(\frac{1}{R_1}+\frac{1}{R_2}\right)^{-1}\\ &=\left(\frac{1}{100}+\frac{1}{300}\right)^{-1}=75\ \Omega\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}I_1&=V_1/R_1=12/100=0.120\ \mathrm{A}\\ I_2&=V_2/R_2=12/300=0.040\ \mathrm{A}\\ I_p&=0.120+0.040=0.160\ \mathrm{A}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}P_1&=V_1I_1=(12)(0.120)=1.44\ \mathrm{W}\\ P_2&=V_2I_2=(12)(0.040)=0.48\ \mathrm{W}\\ P_p&=1.44+0.48=1.92\ \mathrm{W}\end{aligned} \]

      The summed branch current matches \(12/75=0.160\ \mathrm{A}\), and \(12(0.160)=1.92\ \mathrm{W}\) matches the summed dissipation.

      Result

      Series. At 400 Ω, both resistors carry 30 mA. Their drops are 3 V and 9 V and their losses are 0.09 W and 0.27 W, totaling 0.36 W.

      Parallel. At 75 Ω, the source supplies 160 mA. Both branches see 12 V. They draw 120 mA and 40 mA and dissipate 1.44 W and 0.48 W, totaling 1.92 W.
