      Worked example

      With 100 Ω and 300 Ω in series, a 12 V source drives 30 mA through both resistors. The drops of 3 V and 9 V sum to 12 V. In parallel, both branches see 12 V. Their currents of 120 mA and 40 mA add to 160 mA, with an equivalent resistance of 75 Ω.
