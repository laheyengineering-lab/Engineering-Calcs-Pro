      Worked example

      Given

      A series circuit has \(R=10\ \Omega\), \(L=100\ \mathrm{mH}\) and initial current \(I_0=0\ \mathrm{A}\). The source steps to \(V_s=5\ \mathrm{V}\). Evaluate at \(t=10\ \mathrm{ms}\).

      Find

      Find time constant, final current, current and voltages at 10 ms, and energy stored in the inductor.

      Solution

      \[ \tau=\frac{L}{R}=\frac{0.100}{10}=0.010\ \mathrm{s}=10\ \mathrm{ms} \]
      \[ I_\infty=\frac{V_s}{R}=\frac{5}{10}=0.500\ \mathrm{A} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}i(t)&=I_\infty+(I_0-I_\infty)e^{-t/\tau}\\ i(0.010)&=0.500-0.500e^{-1}\\ &\approx0.3161\ \mathrm{A}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}v_R&=iR\approx(0.3161)(10)=3.161\ \mathrm{V}\\ v_L&=V_s-v_R\approx5-3.161=1.839\ \mathrm{V}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}E&=\tfrac12 Li^2\approx\tfrac12(0.100)(0.316060)^2\\ &\approx4.995\ \mathrm{mJ}\end{aligned} \]

      Result

      After 10 ms, current is 0.3161 A and is approaching a steady 0.500 A. The drops are 3.161 V across the resistor and 1.839 V across the inductor, which stores 4.995 mJ.
