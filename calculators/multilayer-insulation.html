    Worked example

    100 mm of insulation at 0.04 W/(m·K) plus material R = 0.20 m²·K/W gives 2.70 m²·K/W. With surface films of 0.13 and 0.04, total R is 2.87 m²·K/W and U is 0.3484 W/(m²·K).
