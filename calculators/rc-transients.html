    Assumptions and limitations

      Worked example

      Given

      A series circuit has \(R=10\ \mathrm{k\Omega}\) and \(C=100\ \mathrm{\mu F}\). The capacitor starts at \(V_0=2\ \mathrm{V}\). At \(t=0\) the source steps to \(V_s=12\ \mathrm{V}\). Evaluate at \(t=1\ \mathrm{s}\).

      Find

      Find the time constant, capacitor and resistor voltages, current, capacitor charge and stored energy.

      Solution

      \[ \begin{aligned}\tau&=RC=(10{,}000)(100\times10^{-6})\\ &=1.00\ \mathrm{s}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}v_C(t)&=V_s+(V_0-V_s)e^{-t/\tau}\\ v_C(1)&=12+(2-12)e^{-1/1}\approx8.321\ \mathrm{V}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}v_R&=V_s-v_C\approx12-8.321\\ &\approx3.679\ \mathrm{V}\\ i&=v_R/R\approx3.679/10{,}000\\ &\approx0.3679\ \mathrm{mA}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}q&=Cv_C\approx(100\times10^{-6})(8.321)\\ &\approx0.8321\ \mathrm{mC}\\ E&=\tfrac12 Cv_C^2\approx\tfrac12(100\times10^{-6})(8.321)^2\\ &\approx3.462\ \mathrm{mJ}\end{aligned} \]

      Result

      At one time constant the capacitor reaches 8.321 V, holds 0.8321 mC and stores 3.462 mJ. The resistor drops 3.679 V at a series current of 0.3679 mA. The capacitor has completed about 63.2% of its rise from 2 V to 12 V.
