Tolerance Stack-Up Calculator

Combine up to five bilateral dimensional tolerance contributions using guaranteed worst-case and statistical root-sum-square methods.

Engineering Theory

\[ T_{WC} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} |t_i|,\qquad T_{RSS} = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} t_i^2} \]

Worst-case addition assumes every contributor reaches its limiting direction at once. RSS combines independent, centered statistical contributors and is an estimate rather than a guaranteed bound.

Variable definitions

  • ti = magnitude of each entered bilateral half-range tolerance
  • TWC = worst-case total half-range
  • TRSS = root-sum-square combined half-range estimate

Assumptions & Limitations

  • Each entry is a non-negative bilateral half-range; contributors are combined by magnitude, regardless of stack direction.
  • All contributors must describe compatible dimensions in the selected units; each field converts independently.
  • Worst-case is a limit for the stated contributors. RSS assumes independent, uncorrelated, centered variation and comparable statistical coverage.
  • RSS is not a guaranteed assembly limit; distributions, correlations, process capability, nominal dimensions, and asymmetric limits are not modeled.

Calculation

Enter each contributor as a positive ± half-range. Leave unused rows blank.

Ready to calculate.

Worked Example

For independent ±0.10 mm, ±0.20 mm, and ±0.15 mm contributors, worst-case is ±0.45 mm. RSS is √(0.10² + 0.20² + 0.15²) = ±0.2693 mm.
