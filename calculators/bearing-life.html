Bearing Life Calculator

Calculate basic rolling-element bearing rating life (L10) from dynamic rating, equivalent load, and speed.

Theory

L10 = (C / P)p
L10h = (L10 × 10⁶) / (60n)

This is a basic rating-life calculation where L10 corresponds to 90% reliability.

Exponents

  • Ball bearings: p = 3
  • Roller bearings: p = 10/3

Notes

  • Basic rating life is a statistical rating, not guaranteed field life.
  • Actual life depends on lubrication, contamination, alignment, installation, load variation, and temperature.
  • Manufacturer methods may include additional factors not used here.
