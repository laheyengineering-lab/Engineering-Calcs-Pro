Bearing Life Calculator

Calculate basic rolling-element bearing rating life (L10) from dynamic rating, equivalent load, and speed.

Theory

L10 = (C / P)p
L10h = (L10 × 10⁶) / (60n)

This is a basic rating-life calculation where L10 corresponds to 90% reliability.

Exponents

  • Ball bearings: p = 3
  • Roller bearings: p = 10/3

Notes

  • Basic rating life is a statistical rating, not guaranteed field life.
  • Actual life depends on lubrication, contamination, alignment, installation, load variation, and temperature.
  • Manufacturer methods may include additional factors not used here.

Worked Example

Given (Ball Bearing):

Dynamic Load Rating: C = 30 kN

Equivalent Dynamic Load: P = 10 kN

Rotational Speed: n = 1,500 rpm

Find: Basic rating life in revolutions and hours

Solution:

L10 = (C / P)p = (30 / 10)3 = 3³ = 27 million revolutions

L10h = (L10 × 10⁶) / (60 × n) = (27 × 10⁶) / (60 × 1,500) = 300 hours

Notes

  • L10 life corresponds to 90% reliability — 10% of a population of identical bearings under identical conditions will fail before this life.
  • Actual service life depends on lubrication quality, contamination level, alignment, temperature, and load variation.
  • Equivalent dynamic load P accounts for combined radial and axial loads using manufacturer X and Y factors — consult bearing data sheets for specific values.
  • Bearing life exponent p = 3 for ball bearings and p = 10/3 for roller bearings per classical ISO 281 rating life.
  • For extended reliability targets (beyond 90%), apply a life modification factor (a1) per ISO 281.
  • This calculator does not apply lubrication, contamination, or application-specific adjustment factors.
