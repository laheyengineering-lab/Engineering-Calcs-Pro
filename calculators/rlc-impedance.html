      Worked example

      With 10 Ω, 100 mH and 100 µF, resonance occurs at about 50.33 Hz. The inductive and capacitive reactances each have magnitude 31.62 Ω and cancel in series. A 10 V RMS source then drives 1 A RMS and dissipates 10 W in the resistor.
