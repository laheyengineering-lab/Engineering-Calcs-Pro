    Assumptions and limitations

      Worked example

      Given

      An ideal series RLC circuit has \(R=10\ \Omega\), \(L=100\ \mathrm{mH}\) and \(C=100\ \mathrm{\mu F}\). Apply a sinusoidal \(10\ \mathrm{V}\) RMS at 100 Hz.

      Find

      Find reactances, complex impedance, magnitude, phase, RMS current and real power at 100 Hz. Compare with resonance.

      Solution

      \[ \omega=2\pi f=2\pi(100)\approx628.32\ \mathrm{rad/s} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}X_L&=\omega L\approx628.3185(0.100)\\ &\approx62.832\ \Omega\\ X_C&=\frac{1}{\omega C}\approx\frac{1}{628.3185(100\times10^{-6})}\\ &\approx15.915\ \Omega\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}Z&=R+j(X_L-X_C)\\ &\approx10+j(62.83185-15.91549)\\ &\approx(10+j46.916)\ \Omega\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}|Z|&=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}\\ &\approx\sqrt{10^2+46.916^2}\\ &\approx47.970\ \Omega\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}\phi&=\arg Z\approx\tan^{-1}\!(46.916/10)\\ &\approx+77.97^\circ\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}I_{\rm rms}&=V_{\rm rms}/|Z|\approx10/47.970\\ &\approx0.2085\ \mathrm{A}\\ P&=I_{\rm rms}^2R\approx(0.20846)^2(10)\\ &\approx0.4346\ \mathrm{W}\end{aligned} \]

      At resonance \(X_L=X_C\), so \(\omega_0^2 LC=1\) and the impedance is purely resistive.

      \[ \begin{aligned}f_0&=\frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}\\ &=\frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{(0.100)(100\times10^{-6})}}\\ &\approx50.33\ \mathrm{Hz}\\ I_{\rm rms,0}&=10/10=1.00\ \mathrm{A}\\ P_0&=(1.00)^2(10)=10.0\ \mathrm{W}\end{aligned} \]

      Result

      At 100 Hz the load is inductive with |Z| = 47.970 Ω. Current lags voltage by 77.97° and the resistor dissipates 0.4346 W at 0.2085 A RMS. At 50.33 Hz the ideal circuit draws 1.00 A RMS in phase with the source and dissipates 10.0 W.
