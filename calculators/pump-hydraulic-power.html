    Worked example

    Given

    In Pressure rise mode, a pump moves water of density \(\rho=1000\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}\) at \(Q=60\ \mathrm{L/min}\) against \(\Delta p=200\ \mathrm{kPa}\). Pump efficiency is \(\eta=80\%\) and operating time \(t=2\ \mathrm{h}\).

    Find

    Find hydraulic output, shaft input power, shaft energy and equivalent head rise.

    Solution

    \[ Q=\frac{60}{60{,}000}=0.001\ \mathrm{m^3/s} \]
    \[ \Delta p=200{,}000\ \mathrm{Pa},\quad \eta=0.80 \]
    \[ \begin{aligned}P_h&=Q\Delta p=(0.001)(200{,}000)\\ &=200\ \mathrm{W}\end{aligned} \]
    \[ P_s=\frac{P_h}{\eta}=\frac{200}{0.80}=250\ \mathrm{W} \]
    \[ \begin{aligned}E_s&=P_st=(0.250\ \mathrm{kW})(2\ \mathrm{h})\\ &=0.50\ \mathrm{kWh}\end{aligned} \]
    \[ H=\frac{\Delta p}{\rho g}=\frac{200{,}000}{1000(9.80665)}\approx20.39\ \mathrm{m} \]

    Result

    The pump delivers 200 W hydraulically at 20.39 m of head. It requires 250 W at the shaft and 0.50 kWh of shaft energy over two hours. Motor and drive losses are outside this estimate.
