    Assumptions and limitations

      Worked example

      Given

      A 12 V DC supply drives a prescribed \(I=5\ \mathrm{A}\) through matching outgoing and return copper conductors, each \(L=10\ \mathrm{m}\) long. In conductor area mode enter \(A=2\ \mathrm{mm^2}\) and \(T=20^\circ\mathrm{C}\). Use the annealed copper preset.

      Find

      Find one-way and loop resistance, voltage drop, load voltage, percent drop and wire loss.

      Solution

      At the 20 °C reference, the preset resistivity is \(\rho=1.7241\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{\Omega\cdot m}\) with no temperature correction.

      \[ A=2(10^{-3})^2=2\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}R_{\rm one}&=\frac{\rho L}{A}=\frac{(1.7241\times10^{-8})(10)}{2\times10^{-6}}\\ &=0.086205\ \Omega\\ R_{\rm loop}&=2R_{\rm one}=0.172410\ \Omega\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}\Delta V&=IR_{\rm loop}=(5)(0.172410)\\ &=0.86205\ \mathrm{V}\\ V_{\rm load}&=12-0.86205=11.13795\ \mathrm{V}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \frac{\Delta V}{V_s}=\frac{0.86205}{12}\approx7.184\% \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}P_{\rm wire}&=I\Delta V=(5)(0.86205)\\ &\approx4.310\ \mathrm{W}\end{aligned} \]

      Result

      The loop drops 0.862 V (7.184%) and leaves 11.138 V at the load. It dissipates 4.310 W at the prescribed current. Cable contacts and self-heating are outside the model.
