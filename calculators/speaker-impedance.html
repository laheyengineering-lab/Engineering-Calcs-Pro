      Worked example

      Given

      One amplifier channel drives nominal \(Z_1=8\ \Omega\) and \(Z_2=16\ \Omega\) speakers in parallel. In RMS output voltage mode, assume the channel maintains \(V_{\rm rms}=12\ \mathrm{V}\).

      Find

      Find equivalent nominal load, RMS channel current, estimated total power and power per speaker.

      Solution

      \[ Z_{\rm eq}=\frac{Z_1Z_2}{Z_1+Z_2}=\frac{8(16)}{8+16}\approx5.333\ \Omega \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}I_1&=12/8=1.50\ \mathrm{A}\\ I_2&=12/16=0.75\ \mathrm{A}\\ I_{\rm rms}&=I_1+I_2=1.50+0.75=2.25\ \mathrm{A}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}P_1&=V_{\rm rms}^2/Z_1=12^2/8=18\ \mathrm{W}\\ P_2&=V_{\rm rms}^2/Z_2=12^2/16=9\ \mathrm{W}\\ P_{\rm total}&=18+9=27\ \mathrm{W}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ V_{\rm rms} I_{\rm rms}=(12)(2.25)=27\ \mathrm{W} \]

      Result

      The nominal load is 5.333 Ω and draws an estimated 2.25 A RMS. The 27 W total divides into 18 W and 9 W. Actual impedances vary with frequency and the amplifier may deliver a different voltage under load.
