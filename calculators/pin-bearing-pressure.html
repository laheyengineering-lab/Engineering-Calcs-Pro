Pin Bearing Pressure Calculator

Estimate average bearing pressure on one plate from the load it carries through a pin or fastener.

Engineering Theory

\[ p_b = \frac{F}{A_p} = \frac{F}{d t} \]

Projected bearing area is pin diameter multiplied by the thickness of the plate carrying the stated load. The result is an average nominal pressure over that projected area.

Variable definitions

  • F = force transferred to this plate
  • d = pin diameter at the bearing interface
  • t = thickness of the loaded plate
  • Ap = projected area, d × t

Assumptions & Limitations

  • The entered load is the portion carried by this individual plate; load sharing between plates is not estimated.
  • Pressure is averaged over projected area and is not the local contact-pressure distribution.
  • Clearance, edge distance, pin bending/shear, tear-out, fatigue, material allowables, and code-specific checks are excluded.
  • This calculation is not a pass/fail strength or safety determination.

Worked Example

A 10 kN plate load on a 10 mm pin through an 8 mm plate gives Ap = 80 mm² and pb = 10,000 N / 80 mm² = 125 MPa.
