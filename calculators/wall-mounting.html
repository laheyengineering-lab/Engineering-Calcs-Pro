    Worked example

    A centered 200 N shelf load at 0.30 m offset with two upper fasteners and bearing 0.15 m below them produces 60 N·m. Idealized shear is 100 N and tension is 200 N per fastener, with 400 N total lower wall bearing.

    Practical application

    A flush hanger primarily sees shear in this idealization. A projecting shelf develops upper-row withdrawal demand. Use the manufacturer's instructions and working capacities for the actual fastener and substrate.
