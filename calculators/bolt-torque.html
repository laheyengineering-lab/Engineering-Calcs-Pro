Bolt Torque Calculator

Calculate the required tightening torque for threaded fasteners based on bolt size, clamp load, and friction factor.

Theory

T = K × D × F

Moment is calculated by multiplying the applied force by the perpendicular distance from the pivot point.

Variables

  • T = Tightening torque
  • K = Nut factor (friction coefficient)
  • D = Nominal bolt diameter
  • F = Desired clamp load

Worked Example

Given:

Force = 100 N

Distance = 25 mm

M = 100 × 25

M = 2500 N·mm

Notes

  • Keep units consistent throughout the calculation.
