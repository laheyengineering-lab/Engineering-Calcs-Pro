Moment Calculator

Calculate the moment (torque) created by a force acting at a perpendicular distance from a pivot point.

Theory

M = F × d

Moment is calculated by multiplying the applied force by the perpendicular distance from the pivot point.

  • F = applied force
  • d = perpendicular distance from pivot
  • M = resulting moment

Calculation

Inputs

Output

Ready to calculate.

Worked Example

Given:

Force = 100 N

Distance = 25 mm

M = 100 × 25

M = 2500 N·mm

Notes

  • Moment depends on perpendicular distance from the pivot.
  • Keep units consistent throughout the calculation.
  • Sign convention depends on the direction of rotation.

Applications

  • Machine design
  • Statics problems
  • Fastener loading
  • Shaft and lever analysis
