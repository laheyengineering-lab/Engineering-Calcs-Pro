Moment Calculator

Calculate the moment (torque) created by a force acting at a perpendicular distance from a pivot point.

Equation

M = F × d

Calculator

Ready to calculate.

Worked Example

Given:

Force = 100 N

Distance = 25 mm

M = 100 × 25

M = 2500 N·mm

Engineering Notes

  • Moment depends on perpendicular distance from the pivot.
  • Keep units consistent throughout the calculation.
  • Sign convention depends on the direction of rotation.

Applications

  • Machine design
  • Statics problems
  • Fastener loading
  • Shaft and lever analysis
