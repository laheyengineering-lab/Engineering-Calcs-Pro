    Worked example

    Given

    A double-acting cylinder with a 100 mm bore \(D\), 60 mm rod \(d\) and 200 mm stroke \(s\) runs at \(n=10\) complete cycles/min. Supply pressure is 500 kPa gauge. Ambient and reference pressure are 100 kPa absolute. Both air temperatures are 20 °C.

    Find

    Find each swept volume, total swept volume, supply absolute pressure, reference air per cycle and average reference flow.

    Solution

    Convert the bore, rod diameter and stroke to 0.100 m, 0.060 m and 0.200 m. Multiply swept volumes in cubic meters by 1000 for liters.

    \[ \begin{aligned}V_c&=\frac{\pi D^2s}{4}=\frac{\pi(0.100)^2(0.200)}{4}\\ &\approx1.5708\ \mathrm{L}\\ V_a&=\frac{\pi(D^2-d^2)s}{4}\\ &=\frac{\pi(0.100^2-0.060^2)(0.200)}{4}\\ &\approx1.0053\ \mathrm{L}\end{aligned} \]
    \[ \begin{aligned}V_s&=V_c+V_a\approx1.5708+1.0053\\ &\approx2.5761\ \mathrm{L}\end{aligned} \]
    \[ \begin{aligned}p_{\rm abs}&=p_g+p_{\rm amb}=500+100\\ &=600\ \mathrm{kPa}\end{aligned} \]
    \[ T_{\rm op}=T_{\rm ref}=20+273.15=293.15\ \mathrm{K} \]

    Use the ideal-gas pressure and temperature ratios for both chambers charged once per cycle.

    \[ \begin{aligned}V_{\rm ref}&=V_s\frac{p_{\rm abs}}{p_{\rm ref}}\frac{T_{\rm ref}}{T_{\rm op}}\\ &\approx(2.5761)\frac{600}{100}\frac{293.15}{293.15}\\ &\approx15.4566\ \mathrm{L/cycle}\end{aligned} \]
    \[ \begin{aligned}Q_{\rm ref}&=nV_{\rm ref}\approx(10)(15.4566)\\ &\approx154.57\ \mathrm{L/min}\end{aligned} \]

    Result

    Ideal consumption is 15.46 L per cycle or 154.57 L/min, both referenced to 100 kPa absolute and 20 °C. Dead volumes and leakage are excluded.
