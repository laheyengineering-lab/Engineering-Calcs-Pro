      Worked example

      Given

      A single-phase sinusoidal load draws \(I_{\rm rms}=5\ \mathrm{A}\) from \(V_{\rm rms}=120\ \mathrm{V}\). Enter power factor 0.8 and Lagging.

      Find

      Find apparent, real and reactive power and the voltage phase relative to current.

      Solution

      \[ S=V_{\rm rms}I_{\rm rms}=(120)(5)=600\ \mathrm{VA} \]
      \[ \phi=\cos^{-1}(0.8)\approx+36.87^\circ \]
      \[ P=S\cos\phi=(600)(0.8)=480\ \mathrm{W} \]
      \[ \begin{aligned}Q&=S\sin\phi=(600)\sqrt{1-0.8^2}\\ &=+360\ \mathrm{var}\end{aligned} \]
      \[ \sqrt{P^2+Q^2}=\sqrt{480^2+360^2}=600\ \mathrm{VA} \]

      Result

      The load uses 480 W of real power, with +360 var reactive and 600 VA apparent power. The positive phase means current lags voltage by 36.87°. The ratio \(480/600=0.8\) recovers the specified power factor.
