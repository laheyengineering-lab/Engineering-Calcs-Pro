Factor of Safety Calculator

Calculate factor of safety using either stress-based or load-based definitions.

Theory

FoS = Strength / Applied Stress
FoS = Failure Load / Applied Load

This calculator reports a ratio only. Required design factors depend on application, uncertainty, failure mode, standards, and consequences of failure.

Interpretation

  • FoS > 1: applied condition is below specified capacity.
  • FoS = 1: applied condition equals specified capacity.
  • FoS < 1: applied condition exceeds specified capacity.
