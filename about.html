About Engineering Calcs Pro

Engineering Calcs Pro is a collection of browser-based calculators for common mechanical engineering and design calculations.

Purpose

The site provides practical, single-purpose calculators for mechanical engineers, designers, machinists, and students. Each calculator runs in your browser as a static page and explains the relationships it uses, so results can be checked and understood rather than treated as a black box.

Types of calculations

The catalog is organized into these categories:

  • Mechanical – for example moment, shaft torsion, beam deflection, beam bending stress, column buckling, bearing life, press fits, and springs.
  • Materials – for example stress and strain, thermal expansion, and factor of safety.
  • Fasteners – for example bolt torque and thread strength.
  • Manufacturing – for example fillet welds.

Some catalog entries may be marked “Coming Soon” and are not yet available.

Finding a calculator

Go to the calculator catalog on the homepage. You can search by name or keyword (for example “torque”, “beam”, or “bolt”) or browse the cards. Each calculator page also links to related calculators.

How each calculator is presented

  • Governing equations – the relationships the calculator evaluates, with symbols defined.
  • Inputs – the quantities you supply, with selectable units where applicable.
  • Assumptions and limitations – the conditions under which the equations are valid.
  • Worked examples – sample calculations that show how to apply the method and check your own results.
  • References – sources for the background of the method. See Engineering References.

Use with engineering judgment

These calculators are tools to support engineering work. They do not replace engineering judgment, applicable codes and standards, or independent review. Please read Methodology & Limitations before relying on a result.
